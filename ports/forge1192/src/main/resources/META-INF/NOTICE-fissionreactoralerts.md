## Mekanism Generators icon element

The mod icon (`branding/fission_reactor_alerts_icon_*.png`, the in-jar
`logo.png`, and the store avatar derived from them) incorporates an
isometric render of Mekanism Generators' `fission_reactor_port_input`
block texture. Mekanism is by Aidan C. Brady and contributors.

The texture used for the render was taken from the published CurseForge
artifact `mekanism-generators-268566-7904061.jar` (Mekanism: Generators
10.7.19.85 for Minecraft 1.21.1), this mod's pinned compile-time
dependency; its bytes are identical to the file at the source revision
above. The texture was rendered isometrically onto a cube and composited
over a procedurally drawn hazard-stripe background to form the icon. The
raw source texture itself is not included in this repository or its JAR.
